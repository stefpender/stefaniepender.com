Robotic Timber Truss Fabrication

Robotic milling of mass timber truss members. Structural joinery machined into full-size stock on a six-axis arm, with a scanning pass to check what actually got cut.

Team
University of Houston, with the Autodesk Technology Center, Boston
Stack
ABB six-axis arm with spindle, RAPID posts, PowerMill

The problem

Timber is not a predictable stock material. It moves, it isn't square, and the joint still has to fit. Machining structural joinery into full-size members means the toolpath has to absorb the difference between the nominal model and the piece actually clamped on the table.

Scan, then cut

The cell scans the member in place before it cuts, so the toolpath is referenced to the piece actually clamped on the table rather than to the nominal model.
