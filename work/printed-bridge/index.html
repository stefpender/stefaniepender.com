Dar: A 5 m 3D-Printed Bridge

A 5 metre pedestrian bridge, robotically fabricated with Dar as a large-format print on a six-axis industrial arm, then instrumented and exhibited as a walkable structure. It carried live public load while streaming sensor data to a digital twin.
