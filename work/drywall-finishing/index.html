Robotic Drywall Finishing

A six-axis arm on a mobile base applying joint compound to wall assemblies, running end to end from scan to finished surface.

Stack
Robots, software, tooling

The problem

Drywall finishing is skilled, repetitive work that is hard to staff. It is also unforgiving: the finished surface has to read as flat under raking light, on walls that are never built quite the way the model says they were.

Approach

The interesting decisions: how the robot located itself against the wall, how the tool stayed compliant against an uneven surface, and what you gave up to get there.
