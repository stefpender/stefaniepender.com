Generative AI & robotics · 2024

Text-to-Production

Part of Adaptive Product Assembly (APA) at Autodesk. Type “a simple chair,” get a physically assembled chair. This pipeline takes a text prompt through a generative 3D model, into a voxel decomposition, into a robot toolpath sequence, and out the other end as a structure two robots actually build, with no human in the loop after the prompt.

The pipeline

The interesting part is where the time goes. Generation dominates; everything between the mesh and the moving robot is effectively free. That ratio is the argument. Once a model can produce buildable geometry, the bottleneck stops being design and starts being the cell.

Assembly

The physical half is where it gets hard: placement tolerance, gripper design, keeping two arms out of each other's way, and sequencing so the structure stays stable as it goes up.
