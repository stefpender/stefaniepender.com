Text-to-Production

Type “a simple chair,” get a physically assembled chair. This pipeline takes a text prompt through a generative 3D model, into a voxel decomposition, into a robot toolpath sequence, and out the other end as a structure two robots actually build — with no human in the loop after the prompt.
