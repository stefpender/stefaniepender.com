Automation that a machine shop can actually own.

I'm VP of Operations at Trener Robotics, deploying AI-enabled robotics into working shops. I came to engineering the long way: business, then design and fabrication at RISD, then CNC machines, then robots at Autodesk. My grandparents owned a machine shop. I see them in a lot of the people I work with now.

Selected work

2021 – 2025

  • Dar: A 5 m 3D-Printed Bridge

    Large-format additive · 2022

    A robotically fabricated 5 metre pedestrian bridge built with Dar. Printed on a six-axis arm, instrumented, and walked on by the public at Autodesk University.

  • Text-to-Production

    Generative AI & robotics · 2024

    A text prompt becomes a mesh, a voxel decomposition, a toolpath sequence, and a structure two robots actually build. No human in the loop after the prompt.

  • Robotic Drywall Finishing

    Construction automation · 2024

    An arm on a mobile base taping and finishing wall assemblies, with compliant tooling for surfaces that are never quite where the model says they are.

  • Automated Housekeeping with Spot

    Field robotics · Skanska · 2022

    A quadruped walking live construction sites for Skanska, across terrain and clutter that change every day.

  • The Arroyo Bridge

    Robotic fabrication · MADWORKSHOP & USC · 2021

    A 25 m single-span pedestrian bridge welded from hundreds of one-off steel members by robot, then trucked across the country and hung over a canyon.

  • Robotic Timber Truss Fabrication

    Robotic fabrication · Univ. of Houston · 2024

    Machining structural joinery into full-size mass timber stock, with a scanning pass to locate the real member before cutting.

  • Contactless Material Assessment

    Sensing & sustainability · 2025

    Thermal, RGB and depth sensing with machine learning to judge whether material in a standing building is worth recovering, without touching it.

  • Robotics Lab & Workcells

    Infrastructure · 2021–2025

    Specifying, integrating, and safety-certifying multi-robot workcells, plus the training that let other people run them.
