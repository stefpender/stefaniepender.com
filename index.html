Automation that a machine shop can actually own.

I'm VP of Operations at Trener Robotics, deploying AI-enabled robotics into working shops. I came to engineering the long way — business, then design and fabrication at RISD, then CNC, then robots at Autodesk. My grandparents owned a machine shop, so none of this is abstract to me.
